# Image assets

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then optimized locally to WebP. No API key or CLI generation fallback was used.

The originals are in `public/images`: `hero.webp`, `hero-panorama.webp`, `dolomites.webp`, `vietnam.webp`, `morocco.webp`, `norway.webp`, `japan.webp`, `newzealand.webp`, `blueridge.webp`, and `patagonia.webp`.

Everything else served from those originals — the narrower widths the layout actually asks for, the 1200x630 share cards, the app icons and the maskable icon — is derived by `npm run assets` and committed alongside them. Re-run it after replacing a photo or the mark rather than editing the derived files.

The shared production brief was premium cinematic editorial motorcycle travel photography, realistic natural landscape textures, restrained natural colours, no labels, logos, UI or watermarks. The destination scenes were Dolomite switchbacks, Ha Giang karst roads, High Atlas canyon roads, Lofoten island bridges, Aso volcanic grasslands, Lake Pukaki and the Southern Alps, autumn Blue Ridge roads, and gravel roads beside Lago General Carrera. The final desktop hero specifically requested a 3:1 panorama with both the mountain peaks and the rider visible inside the shallow frame, with a naturally darker left side for code-native white copy. The mobile hero retains a taller scenic composition.

The UI concept briefs requested the complete desktop discovery screen and coordinated mobile discovery/detail screens for ROAM, using warm white, forest green, orange arrows, strong sans-serif type, overlapping search, large destination photography, route facts, native controls and a bottom mobile navigation.

All destination photography is illustrative AI-generated imagery and is disclosed in the app footer and About panel.
